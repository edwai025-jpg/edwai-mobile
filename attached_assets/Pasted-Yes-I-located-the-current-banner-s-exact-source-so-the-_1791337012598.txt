Below 768 px: two-row arrangement, as in your screenshot.
768 px and above: information chips and the subscription widget move into the top row.
Below 640 px: email/phone and Profile/Logout labels are hidden.
Wider layouts can also show State, Medium, institute details and subscription-period information.
If an institute supplies a primary colour, that colour replaces the default purple background gradient. Institute logo/name overrides must also be preserved.

One implementation detail: although the source includes a sticky class, an inline position: relative overrides it. Do not describe the existing banner as reliably sticky.
