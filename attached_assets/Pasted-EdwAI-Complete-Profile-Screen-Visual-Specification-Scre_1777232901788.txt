EdwAI — Complete Profile Screen (Visual Specification)
Screen Background

Light airy gradient — white center fading to very soft blue-purple at edges. Think linear-gradient(135deg, #EEF2FF → #FFFFFF → #F5F3FF). No visible texture; the card floats on top.
Overall Card

    Shape: Single tall white card, centered horizontally, with softly rounded corners (~16px radius) and a very subtle box shadow
    Width: Full-width on mobile with ~16px horizontal padding on each side
    Padding inside card: ~24–32px top, ~16–20px sides
    No visible card border

Header (top of card)
Badge (topmost element)

    Small horizontal pill, centered
    Light indigo/purple fill with a slight border
    Left dot: small filled indigo circle (~6px)
    Text: "SETUP YOUR PROFILE" — uppercase, tiny (10–11px), medium weight, indigo color
    Resembles a status indicator chip

Title

    "Welcome to EdwAI" — large, bold (~28–30px), centered
    "Edw" = indigo/violet color (#4F46E5 range)
    "AI" = dark charcoal/black
    Single line on most phones

Subtitle

    "Tell us about yourself to personalise your experience"
    Gray text (~13px), centered, below title
    ~6–8px gap from title

Section 1 Block
Section Number Badge

    Filled circle (~28px), indigo/purple background
    White number "1" inside, centered, bold
    A thin horizontal divider line extends from the circle's right edge to the card's right edge

Field: Your Name

    Label: "Your Name" — ~14px, dark gray, left-aligned, ~8px above input
    Input field: full width, rounded corners (~8px), light gray border, white/very light gray fill
    Placeholder: "Enter your full name" — medium gray
    Height: ~48px

Divider

    Thin horizontal rule (border-gray-100 or similar) spanning full card width, ~8–12px vertical margin

Field: Mobile Number (optional)

    Label row: "Mobile Number" (dark, ~14px bold) + small gray pill badge with text "optional" — rounded pill, gray bg, ~11px font, positioned right of the label text
    Description below label: "Add your Indian mobile number to enable phone OTP login and get it pre-filled during payment." — light gray, ~12px, 2 lines
    Input (split): left gray box with "+91" (non-editable, ~44px wide, medium gray bg, left-rounded corners) + main text input right portion with placeholder "10-digit mobile number" (right-rounded corners, white/light gray fill)
    Combined height: ~48px
    Below the input — two items on same row:
        Left: "This is optional — you can always add it later." in light gray, ~11px
        Right: "Skip for now" — small link text, indigo/purple color, ~11px

Section 2 Block
Section Number Badge

    Same style as Section 1 — filled indigo circle with "2" + extending horizontal divider line

Find Your Board

Label: "Find Your Board" — ~16px, dark, bold, left-aligned

State Dropdown:

    Label: "State" — ~12–13px, medium gray, left-aligned
    Dropdown control: full-width, ~48px tall, rounded (~8px), light gray border, white fill
    Text: "Select State" — gray placeholder text, left-aligned
    Right side: standard dropdown chevron arrow (▼) — gray, right-aligned inside the field

Terms Checkbox Row

    A small unchecked square checkbox (~16×16px) on the left
    Text to its right (wraps to 2 lines): "I agree to EdwAI's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, including the use of AI to generate answers from textbook content."
        "Terms of Service" and "Privacy Policy" are indigo/blue hyperlinks, rest is ~12–13px gray text
    The entire row sits inside a very light blue-gray rounded box (bg-blue-50 or similar, ~10px radius, subtle border)
    ~16px padding inside, full width

"Use Your Own Resources" Card (below the terms row)

Visually distinct section with a light green-tinted background card:

    Background: Very light green/mint (bg-green-50 or rgba(16,185,129,0.05))
    Border: Thin light green border or none
    Rounded corners: ~12px
    Padding: ~16px all around

Content layout (top row): Icon left, text right

    Icon: Small green upload/book icon (SVG) — ~24–28px, green color, positioned top-left of the card
    Title: "Use Your Own Resources" — ~14px, dark/charcoal, bold
    Description: "Upload your own textbooks or YouTube videos and start studying right away." — ~12px, gray
    Below that (separate line): "Fill in your name and select a board above first." — ~11–12px, lighter gray, italic feel

Button: Full-width, rounded pill shape (~24–28px radius), inside this same card

    Current state (disabled): Muted green background (bg-green-300 or washed-out green), white text
    Text: "Proceed to Own Books" — centered, ~14px, medium-bold weight
    When enabled (name + board filled): becomes a solid bright green (#10B981 or similar)

Footer (below the card)

    Centered line with green shield checkmark icon (✅ or green circle check) + text: "Trusted by 10,000+ students across India"
    ~12–13px, dark gray
    ~16px padding below
